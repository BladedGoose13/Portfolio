About

I'm a second-year Engineering Physics student at Tecnológico de Monterrey, with a focus on computational materials science and scientific computing.

My research centers on first-principles modeling of low-dimensional quantum and semiconductor materials using Density Functional Theory. Currently studying Janus monolayer systems for spintronic applications under the guidance of Dr. José Angel Reyes Retana.

I've studied in Korea (SKKU), fabricated devices at MIT.nano, and am heading to the National Institute for Materials Science in Tsukuba, Japan for a summer research internship.

I value interdisciplinarity deeply - complementing my technical background with credentials in project management and business administration, and building systems that connect computation, materials science, and real-world impact.
