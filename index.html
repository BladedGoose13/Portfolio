About Me

I am an Engineering Physics undergraduate at Tecnológico de Monterrey, specializing in scientific computing and materials informatics.

My current research focuses on the structural, electronic, and optical properties of siligraphene monolayers through first-principles calculations using Density Functional Theory with Quantum ESPRESSO.

I am actively building expertise at the intersection of ab-initio methods and physics-informed machine learning, with a particular emphasis on PINNs (Physics-Informed Neural Networks) and data-driven surrogate models for semiconductor device simulation and functional materials discovery.

Research Focus

Bridging first-principles simulations and data-driven methods for next-generation materials

Siligraphene Monolayers (DFT)

First-principles analysis of Si–C two-dimensional allotropes using Density Functional Theory. Investigating electronic band structure, density of states, and optical response with Quantum ESPRESSO.

  • Quantum ESPRESSO
  • DFT
  • 2D Materials
  • Band Structure

Semiconductor TCAD

Technology Computer-Aided Design for semiconductor devices. Multiphysics process and device simulation combining drift-diffusion, carrier transport, and quantum corrections for advanced node modeling.

  • TCAD
  • Drift-Diffusion
  • Device Simulation
  • FinFET / MOSFET

Physics-Informed Neural Networks

Developing PINNs and neural operator architectures that embed governing PDEs (Schrödinger, Poisson, drift-diffusion) directly into the loss functional, enabling mesh-free surrogate models for materials and device physics.

  • PINNs
  • Neural Operators
  • PDEs
  • PyTorch

Materials Informatics

Data-driven discovery and characterization of functional materials using ML interatomic potentials, graph neural networks on crystal structures, and high-throughput screening pipelines integrated with DFT databases.

  • Graph Neural Nets
  • MLIP
  • High-Throughput DFT
  • Materials Project

Featured Projects

Computational research projects and open-source contributions

DFT

Siligraphene Electronic Structure

DFT study of SiC-based 2D monolayers exploring band gap tunability, effective mass anisotropy, and phonon stability. Pseudopotential generation and convergence tests with Quantum ESPRESSO pw.x / ph.x.

Quantum ESPRESSO Python ASE pymatgen
PINN

PINN for Poisson–Schrödinger System

Physics-Informed Neural Network solving the coupled Poisson–Schrödinger equations in a 1D quantum-well heterostructure. Self-consistent loop embedded in the PINN loss with automatic differentiation via PyTorch.

PyTorch Python NumPy Matplotlib
TCAD

ML-Assisted Semiconductor TCAD

Surrogate model trained on TCAD simulation data to predict I–V characteristics and threshold voltage shifts. Reduces full TCAD simulation time while preserving physical accuracy for process variation analysis.

scikit-learn PyTorch Sentaurus Python
Informatics

High-Throughput 2D Materials Screening

Automated DFT workflow using pymatgen + FireWorks to screen 2D materials from the C2DB database. Filters for dynamical stability, non-trivial topology, and bandgap range suitable for photovoltaic applications.

pymatgen FireWorks DFT Python
